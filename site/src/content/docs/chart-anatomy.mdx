A chart fills its container (see [Sizing and layout](/snaplot/sizing/)). Inside it, the **plot**
is where the marks draw, and four **gutters** around the plot hold the axes: tick marks, tick
labels and titles. A legend takes a strip above or below them.

The chart below draws its own layout over itself, measured from the chart as it rendered. Change
its values and axes to see each gutter follow what it holds, and point at an entry in the key to
find its region.

## One rule for every side

Each gutter is measured from what it holds, and every side follows the same rule:

```
gutter = max(minimum, round up(8 + max(own axis, overhang)))
```

- **Own axis** is the axis on that side, if it has one: a 4 px tick mark, a 4 px gap and its tick
  labels, plus an 8 px gap and a line of text when it has a title. On the left and right the
  widest label counts; on the top and bottom, one line of text.
- **Overhang** is how far the other axis's end labels reach past the plot. Labels are centred on
  their ticks, so the first and last x labels reach half their width into the left and right
  gutters, and the y labels half their height into the top and bottom. It is reserved whether or
  not a tick sits on the end, so the plot keeps its size while you pan, zoom or stream.
- **8** is the edge margin: clear space between the outermost text and the edge of the chart, or
  the edge of its legend.
- **Round up** goes to the next whole pixel. Only the overhang into the left and right goes to
  the next 8 px: it follows whichever x label sits at the end, which changes as you pan, and the
  step keeps the plot's width through most of those changes.
- **Minimum** is `minGutters.y` on a side that holds a y axis and `minGutters.x` on a side that
  holds an x axis, 0 unless you set it. A side with no axis has no minimum.

Two y axes take the same room: when there is a second y axis on the right, the left and right
gutters both take the wider of the two, so the plot stays centred between its axes. In a group
that lines up its plots, each side can widen on its own to match the other members.

| Side | Own axis | Overhang |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | The y axis: its widest tick label, tick, gap and title | Half the first x label |
| Right | A second y axis, then a heatmap's slice panel and colour scale | Half the last x label |
| Top | An x axis placed on top | Half a y label's height |
| Bottom | The x axis: a line of tick labels, tick, gap and title | Half a y label's height |

The plot gets whatever room is left. A chart with short labels has a wider plot, and its y
labels start the edge margin from the edge, to within a pixel, whatever they say. They sit
further in only when the first x label reaches further into the gutter than they do.

## Lining up plots

Two charts get the same space before their labels, but the one with longer labels needs a wider
gutter, so their plots start at different places. To line the plots up, give both charts the
same left and right gutters.

<PlotAlignment />

- **In a group**, `alignPlots` (on by default) gives every member the widest member's left and
  right gutters. See [Linked charts](/snaplot/guides/linked-charts/).
- **Outside a group**, give each chart the same `minGutters.y`. Pick a width that holds the
  widest labels you expect: a gutter never narrows below what it holds, so a wider label still
  moves its plot.

```ts
createChart(lossElement, { ...lossOptions, minGutters: { y: 80 } });
createChart(throughputElement, { ...throughputOptions, minGutters: { y: 80 } });
```

## Minimum gutters

`minGutters` sets the least room the gutters that hold the axes keep, in CSS pixels:

- `y` is the width of each gutter that holds a y axis: the left, and the right too when there is
  a second y axis, since two y axes take the same room.
- `x` is the height of the gutter that holds the x axis: the bottom, or the top for an x axis
  placed on top.

A gutter that already needs more keeps what it needs, and a side with no axis keeps no minimum.
Besides lining charts up, a minimum keeps a gutter steady while an axis's labels change length.

## Legend and panels

- **The legend** takes a strip at the top, or at the bottom with `legend: { position: 'bottom' }`.
  The gutters are measured from the strip, so the edge margin sits between the legend and the
  plot's top labels.
- **A heatmap's colour scale** sits at the right edge of the chart, outside any right axis.
- **A heatmap's slice panel** sits beside the plot (120 px by default) or above it (64 px), next to
  the bin axis it shares. It gives way when the plot would be left with less than one and a half
  times its size.
