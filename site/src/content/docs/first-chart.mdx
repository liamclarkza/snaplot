## Mount a chart

Give `createChart` an element and the options: the rows, the field for the x axis, and one series per y field. The React and Solid `<Chart>` components take the same options as props.

<Example name="start/minimal" />

## Size the container

The chart fills its container. Give the container a height, or pass `height`.

## What you get by default

The theme follows the page, axes and ticks come from the data, and the tooltip follows the pointer.
