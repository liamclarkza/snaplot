`type: 'scatter'` draws a point at each row. `colorBy` colours points by a field: strings and a
few distinct integers become categories with a legend entry each, other numbers a ramp. The
tooltip of a point names its x and y, then the colour category, then `tooltipFields` in order.

<Example name="chart-types/scatter" />

## Sized points

`sizeBy` scales each point's area by a field, between the radii of `range` in CSS pixels.

<Example name="chart-types/scatter-sized" />

## Density

Above 200,000 points in view, `renderMode: 'auto'` (the default) draws a density map instead of
points, coloured by how many fall in each cell; zooming in far enough draws the points again.
`'points'` and `'density'` pin one or the other. See [Performance](/snaplot/guides/performance/).
