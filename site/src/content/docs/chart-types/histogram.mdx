`histogram(values)` bins numbers on nice edges and returns bins that a `type: 'histogram'`
series reads as its `data`. Pass `{ bins }` for a bin count or a rule (`'sturges'`, `'scott'`), or
`{ thresholds }` for edges of your own.

<Example name="chart-types/histogram" />

## Two sets on the same bins

To compare two sets, bin both on the same edges: pass the same `thresholds` to each, or one
histogram's `edges` to the other.
