`type: 'area'` draws a line and fills it down to zero, in the line's own `interpolation`. Its
fill is a translucent ramp of its colour; `fill` sets your own colour, and `fill: null` keeps the
outline only. A line beside it reads as a line: here the import is dashed.

<Example name="chart-types/area" />

## Above and below zero

The fill always runs to zero, so values on both sides of it fill up to zero and down to it. A Y
axis of an area that stays on one side starts at zero.
