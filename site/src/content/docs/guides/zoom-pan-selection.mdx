## Interaction presets

`interaction` sets zoom, pan and selection together.

## Mouse

Wheel, drag and double-click.

## Touch

Swipe, drag, tap and pinch.

## Keyboard

Arrow keys move the cursor; Shift with an arrow pans; plus and minus zoom.

## Viewport, selection and highlight

Read and set them as options, with a change callback each.
