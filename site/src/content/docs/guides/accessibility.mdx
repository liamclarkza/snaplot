## Names

The chart's container is a figure named by `ariaLabel`, or by a summary of its series ("Line
chart, 2 series: High, Low") when you give none. Write `ariaLabel` as you would a caption: what
the chart shows and over what range. Attributes the container already has are kept, and
`destroy()` removes only what the chart added. The React and Solid components render the role
and the name on their own element, so server-rendered markup is already a named figure.

## Keyboard

An interactive chart is one tab stop, named by its label and a short key hint. The arrow keys move
the cursor point by point; Home and End jump to the first and last point; Shift with an arrow
pans; plus and minus zoom; 0 resets; Escape clears the selection and the tooltip. Ctrl, ⌘ and Alt
combinations pass through to the browser. The full table is on
[Zoom, pan and selection](/snaplot/guides/zoom-pan-selection/#keyboard).

## Focus and live announcements

Keyboard focus draws a 2 px ring in the theme's accent colour; a mouse click focuses the chart
without one. As the keyboard moves the cursor, a polite live region reads the x and every
series' value, at most once every 250 ms. Canvases, tick labels and the tooltip are hidden from
assistive technology, so a screen reader hears the chart's name and the announcements, not a
list of tick numbers.

## Contrast and colour

Text and axis labels are at least 4.5:1 on their surface. The categorical palettes are chosen to
stay distinct with the common colour-vision deficiencies; when colour carries meaning, say it in
words too, in a label, a legend or the surrounding text.

## Forced colours

In Windows high-contrast modes the focus ring uses the system highlight colour, and legend
swatches and axis labels keep their colours, since the canvas they describe keeps its own.

## Recipe: a data table

A chart is a summary. When readers need exact values, put a table of the data next to it, or
behind a disclosure, with the same caption as `ariaLabel`. A
[legend table](/snaplot/guides/tooltips-legends/#legend-tables) shows the values at the cursor.

## Minimum font size

Axis labels honour the browser's minimum font size and any `font-size` the page forces on them:
the chart measures them at the size they render and fits fewer ticks when they grow.
