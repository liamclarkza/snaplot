Every chart has an `x` and a `y` axis. `axes` configures them by key, and any other key (`y2`,
`x2`) adds an axis that series name with `yAxis` or `xAxis`.

## Axis types

| `type` | Values | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `'linear'` | numbers | The default for numbers. |
| `'log'` | positive numbers | Values at or below 0 are not drawn. |
| `'time'` | epoch milliseconds, `Date`s or ISO 8601 strings | Ticks fall on calendar boundaries in the chart's `timeZone`. |
| `'category'` | strings | One slot per label, in order of first appearance, or in the order `categories` gives. |

Without a `type`, the x values decide (see [Data](/snaplot/guides/data/#axis-inference)).

## Bounds

Automatic bounds come from the data. A value axis rounds them out to a tick step (`nice`). The x
of a line, area or band ends at the data, or at a round value just past it (within 5% of the
span): 0–119 reads 0–120, but 1940–2025 stops at 2025. `nice: true` always rounds. Data that is all positive or all negative never
crosses zero, and an area or a bar always includes zero.

`min` and `max` fix a bound; give one and the other stays automatic. `nice: false` keeps the
data's own extent.

## Ticks

Ticks are chosen for the space the axis has. Labels are measured as they render (in the axis
font, with tabular figures, at the size the page shows them), and when they would collide the
axis takes fewer ticks with a coarser step, so a time axis that runs out of room says `09:00`,
not `09:00:00.0`. Category labels thin to every second, third or nth label that fits, and a long
axis title is cut to its edge with an ellipsis.

### Explicit ticks

`ticks` gives exact tick values (numbers or `Date`s), which the axis keeps as given: the way to
get 0, 50 and 100 on a percentage axis, or only the ends of a sparkline. Gridlines follow them.

## Formatting values

`format` writes tick labels and the values the tooltip, the cursor and legend tables show:

- **numbers:** an `Intl.NumberFormat` options object, plus `prefix` and `suffix`; or a function;
- **times:** an `Intl.DateTimeFormat` options object, or a function of epoch milliseconds.

A series `format` wins over its axis for that series.

## Titles

`title` draws the axis name outside its tick labels, cut with an ellipsis when it is longer than
its side of the plot.

## Positions

`position` moves an axis to any side. Keys starting with `x` default to the bottom, `y` to the
left, and a second value axis to the right.

## Dual axes

A series with `yAxis: 'y2'` reads its own axis, and gets its own range and format. Use it for
two quantities in different units, and say in each axis title which is which.
