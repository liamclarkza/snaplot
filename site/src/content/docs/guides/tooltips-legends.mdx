## Tooltip modes

Show every series at the cursor, or the nearest point.

## Custom tooltip content

`tooltip.render` returns a string or a DOM node. Escape any text you did not write.

## The legend

A legend appears for two or more series; turn it off with `legend: false`.

## Legend tables

`createLegendTable` and `<LegendTable>` show columns of values for the cursor position.

## A custom legend

Build your own with `useCursor` in React or `createCursor` in Solid.
