## Line

The default series type.

## Area

A line filled down to zero.

## Band

A range between a lower and an upper field.

## Bar

Bars on a category or numeric x axis.

## Histogram

Counts per bin from `histogram()`.

## Scatter

Points, with density for large sets, `colorBy` for colour and `sizeBy` for size.
