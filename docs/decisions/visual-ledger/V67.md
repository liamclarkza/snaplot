# V67: one rule sizes every gutter

- **Commit:** `feat(layout): one rule sizes every gutter, and minGutters sets the axes' minimums`
- **Milestone:** none: after 0.15.0
- **Fixtures:** every shot (141 of 141 against `1ab5e24`): each chart's plot moves or changes size.
- **Before → after:**
  - Before: the gutters started from fixed floors (left 56, right 24, top 24, bottom 44 px; top 12 under a legend) and only grew past them. Most y labels are under 40 px wide, so the left gutter was 56 px whatever they said, and the space before the labels changed with their length (17 px before `0.016`, 41 px before `0`). The top was not measured, and the bottom kept 12 px more than a line of labels needs.
  - After: every side is the 8 px edge margin plus the larger of its own axis (tick, gap, labels, title) and the overhang of the other axis's end labels, rounded up (left and right to 8 px), with no floor. At the fixtures' 11 px labels a typical chart's gutters go from 56 / 24 / 24 / 44 to 48 / 24 / 15 / 32 (left, right, top, bottom), and short y labels such as `0 1 2` give a 24 px left gutter. The plot grows by that much. The space before the y labels is now 8 px plus at most 7 px of rounding on every chart. The top is measured as half a label's height plus the edge margin, also under a legend (12 → 15 px there). The end x labels' overhang is reserved whether or not a tick sits on the end, so a pan no longer widens the right gutter on its first frame. Category axes still reserve no overhang: their end labels sit inside their slots, and a long one is clamped into the chart as before.
  - The geometry project (565 checks of label boxes against the canvas edges and each other) passes unchanged.
