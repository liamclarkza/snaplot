- **Commit:** `fix(data): harden histogram binning`
- **Milestone:** M7
- **Fixtures:** `marks/histogram`, `marks/basic-histogram` (and `marks/basic-histogram@2x`),
  `marks/histogram-fill`, `marks/grid-histogram-1-320x200`, `marks/grid-histogram-1-640x320`,
  `marks/grid-histogram-1-1200x480`, `marks/grid-histogram-3-320x200`,

  `marks/grid-histogram-8-640x320`, `marks/grid-histogram-8-1200x480`
- **Before → after:**
  - Before: `histogram(values)` split `[min, max]` into the Freedman–Diaconis count of equal bins,
    so edges and X labels fell on the data's own extremes: `marks/histogram` labels
    `10.5, 38.1, 65.7, 93.3` under bins of width 2.76, and the three overlaid series of
    `marks/grid-histogram-3-640x320` label `41.0, 68.1, 83.6, 99.1, 114.6, 130.1, 145.6, 161.1`,
    each series on its own edges.
  - After: the Freedman–Diaconis width is rounded up to 1, 2, 2.5 or 5 × 10ⁿ and the edges are its
    multiples (dxtrial-14), capped at 1,000 bins (dataapi-06). `marks/histogram` has bins of width 5
    labelled `10, 40, 70, 100`, with the Y axis to 1,000; the overlaid series share
    5-unit edges labelled `40, 60, 80, … 180`, so their bars line up. Bars are wider and taller
    (fewer bins), the bimodal shapes are unchanged. Explicit `bins: n` keeps even edges from the
    smallest to the largest value, so `axes/histogram-tiny-range` does not change.
  - 14 shots of 137 change against the merge base, besides V17's. No baselines are committed
    (only M3a and M14 commit them).
- **Finding:** `dxtrial-14` (with `dataapi-06` and `dataapi-19`)
