- **Before → after:**
  - A linear X axis of lines, areas and bands was rounded out to the next 1/2/5 tick on both sides, though `nice` was documented as off for them: 0–11 ran to 12, 0–89 to 100 and a year axis of 1940–2025 to 2040, a stretch with no data at the end of the plot. A side now rounds out only when the round value is within 5% of the span past the data (0–119 still reads 0–120); otherwise it stops at the data. `nice: true` rounds both sides as before, `nice: false` neither. Scatter X axes and every Y axis keep their round bounds; bars and histograms keep their slots and bins. Asked for by Liam after the chart-type docs' year axis ran to 2040.
